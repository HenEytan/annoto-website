Pricing Questions

Can we start with a pilot?

Yes — start with a free pilot on your own courses, then roll out when you're ready.

What counts as a learner?

Anyone who engages with an Annoto-enabled video during your billing period. Viewers who only watch don't count against your plan.

Do you offer education and non-profit pricing?

Yes. Talk to our team about institution-wide licensing and non-profit rates.
