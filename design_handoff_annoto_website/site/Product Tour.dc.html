Product Tour

Five Minutes Inside Annoto

From the learner’s first comment to the instructor’s analytics view — a walk through the whole experience, in order.

Skip Ahead — Book A Live Demo
01 The Learner Experience

Watching Becomes A Conversation

Learners open the video they were assigned — and find a discussion panel living right on the timeline. Every comment is anchored to a moment.

✓ Time-Anchored Comments — click a comment, jump to its moment.
✓ Personal Notes — private annotations alongside the public discussion.
✓ Any Device — the same experience on laptop, tablet, and phone.
02 Assessment

Understanding, Checked On The Timeline

Quizzes and reflection points pause the video at the moments that matter. Scores flow back to the gradebook automatically.

✓ In-Video Quizzes — multiple choice and open response, placed by the instructor.
✓ Reflection Points — prompts that ask learners to think, not just click.
✓ Gradebook Sync — results post to your LMS via LTI Advantage.
03 Instructor Tools

Teach The Class You Can Finally See

Instructors moderate discussion, answer at the exact moment of confusion, and set completion criteria — all from inside the course.

✓ Moderation & Roles — pin answers, manage discussion, per video.
✓ Peer Review — structured in-video feedback between learners.
✓ Completion Rules — define what “watched” actually means.
04 Analytics

Every Video Tells You What Worked

Attention, comprehension, and participation — per video, per cohort, per learner. Exportable to your reporting stack.

✓ Attention Curves — where learners watch, rewind, and drop off.
✓ Comprehension Signals — quiz performance mapped to the timeline.
✓ Exports & APIs — CSV, xAPI, and dashboards for your data team.
05 Lumo AI

Your Copilot For Video Learning

Lumo drafts quiz questions from the video itself, summarizes discussion, and flags the moments that need an instructor’s eye.

✓ Auto-Drafted Questions — assessment suggestions from the content.
✓ Discussion Summaries — the class conversation, distilled.
✓ Moments That Matter — confusion hotspots surfaced automatically.
